# International Launch Scope

## Current Allowlist

The current engineering scope is a Korean full-service UAT environment. International production countries remain disabled until each country passes the enablement gate below.

## Country Enablement Gate

Before adding a country, the owner must document:

- recruitment/placement licensing and permitted fee model;
- supported jobs and visa information disclaimer;
- language, currency, tax, payment, cancellation, refund, and dispute rules;
- privacy notice, lawful basis, rights process, processors, and transfer mechanism;
- sanctions, fraud, age, consumer, employment, and anti-discrimination requirements;
- counsel approval and an entry in `EXTERNAL_APPROVAL_REGISTER.md`.

Countries are enabled only through a reviewed allowlist change. “Worldwide” launch is prohibited.
