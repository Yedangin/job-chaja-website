# Privacy Rights Procedure

## Intake

Accept access, correction, deletion, withdrawal, objection, and processing-suspension requests through the published privacy contact. Create a restricted support ticket and acknowledge receipt.

## Verification and Handling

1. Verify identity using the minimum information necessary.
2. Record request type, jurisdiction, received date, owner, due date, and systems affected.
3. Search the data map and notify relevant processors.
4. Export, correct, restrict, or delete data as legally permitted.
5. Explain any lawful refusal, appeal route, and retention exception.
6. Record completion evidence without retaining unnecessary identity documents.

## Operational Rules

- Account deletion requests trigger the existing soft-delete process; legal retention exceptions must be documented.
- Marketing withdrawal must take effect immediately and remain in consent history.
- Security incidents discovered during a request are escalated under `OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md`.
- Privacy Lead reviews the request register monthly.
