# Job Advertisement Review Policy

## Launch Rule

Every employer and job advertisement must be reviewed before publication. Automated approval remains disabled for the full-service launch.

## Employer Review

- Confirm legal entity name, business registration, representative, address, and reachable contact.
- Confirm the employer is authorized to recruit for the advertised role.
- Escalate overseas placement, worker-paid fees, deposits, and visa sponsorship claims to Legal.
- Reject impersonation, unverifiable entities, sanctions matches, and repeated abuse.

## Advertisement Review

- Reject discriminatory, illegal, misleading, duplicate, or fee-charging advertisements.
- Require salary/range, workplace, duties, working hours, contract type, and application deadline.
- Treat visa guidance as general information only; never promise approval.
- Record reviewer, timestamp, decision, reason, and evidence.

## Complaints and Removal

- Support immediately hides credible fraud, safety, exploitation, or discrimination reports.
- Operations investigates within one business day and retains an audit record.
- Legal and the incident lead are notified for suspected crime, trafficking, or data exposure.
