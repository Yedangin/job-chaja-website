# Automated Matching and Human Review

## User Notice

Visa and job matching results are informational recommendations generated from user-provided information and configured rules. They are not immigration, legal, employment, or government decisions and do not guarantee eligibility or approval.

## Human Review and Objection

1. Users may request human review through customer support.
2. Support records the disputed result, user explanation, and relevant rule version.
3. Operations pauses any adverse automated action while reviewing.
4. A qualified reviewer explains or corrects the result.
5. Rule defects are escalated to Engineering and Legal and recorded in the change log.

No user may be rejected from the platform solely by an automated matching result.
