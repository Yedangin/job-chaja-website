# Cookie and Analytics Inventory

## Current Launch Scope

| Item | Purpose | Classification | Launch Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authentication session cookie | Keep a signed-in session | Strictly necessary | Allowed |
| Language preference | Remember Korean/English preference | Functional | Allowed |
| Vercel Analytics package | Traffic measurement | Non-essential analytics | Must remain disabled until approved |
| Advertising/remarketing cookies | Marketing | Non-essential marketing | Not implemented / prohibited |

Non-essential analytics or marketing scripts must not be enabled until the Privacy Lead approves the notice, retention, vendors, transfer basis, and consent-preference UI.
